Twelve ways the bands could move

The blue wayfinding band and the one red act band currently arrive flat and stay flat. Each direction below gives that field a press event instead — it prints, rather than simply being there. All twelve run on the three majors only (blue, red, yellow), hold radius 0 and the 2px/3px ink rules, and animate transform and clip only — never opacity. A silkscreen either printed or it didn't.

Readability is structural here, not a setting. Every band's base is unprinted stock and the colour is laid on it, with the type sitting above in ink. Mid-run the type is ink-on-cream; landed it is ink-on-colour. There is no frame where the content is washed out, so the "fun" and the "readable" aren't trading against each other.

Drive the console. Switch the ink to see each direction do the blue hero, the red act band and a yellow notice; slow it to 0.5× to read the mechanics; flip Motion to Reduced to check the contract every one of them has to honour.

Where it went. Three were kept — 01 Press Run, 05 Halftone Bloom and 06 Cut Stack — and rebuilt as ambient loops rather than one-shot entrances, with the grid, timing, stagger order and colour all made adjustable. They now live on band-motion-lab.html, which is the bench for tuning them. This page stays as the record of all twelve.

What all twelve hold to

  • Three majors only. Blue, red, yellow on ink and stock. No minor takes a band job, and no two inks are ever blended — the overlaps are hard-edged plates in z-order, so a fourth colour can't appear.
  • Transform and clip, never opacity. Every move is scale, translate or a hard clip edge. Nothing anywhere fades.
  • The end state is the default. The animation only exists once JS adds .run, so the prerendered HTML, a crawler and a reduced-motion visitor all get the landed band — never a blank one.
  • Type stays ink the whole way. The one exception is red's display type, which re-creams on landing rather than at the start; cream on unprinted stock would be a hole mid-run.

How to read the three kinds

Entrance
Plays once when the band scrolls in, then it is a flat band forever. Highest wow, zero standing cost, and it is the only kind that can carry the hero.
Ambient
Runs forever. Has to stay off the content and stay cheap — both here are edge furniture, not field motion.
Interactive
Driven by scroll or pointer. Best on a band you pass through rather than one you stop and read.

Where these landed

01 Press Run, 05 Halftone Bloom and 06 Cut Stack were kept, and were wanted as loops rather than entrances. They were rebuilt that way on band-motion-lab.html — same three moves, running continuously, with every dial exposed: grid, cycle, hold, stagger order, easing, and colour as either a sequence or a set of weights.

The other nine stay here as the record of the exploration, not as anything you can select. Still nothing adopted: the live bands (.b-wayfind, .b-act, .b-notice in src/index.css) are untouched.

Turning a loop into a shipped band is small from there: the engine becomes a module, the tuned config becomes a constant per band, and the field layer plus the pause-when-offscreen observer go into Hero.jsx and DarkCTA.jsx.
