Five directions for the daily card

The card that goes out alone, every morning. Each direction below gives the day colour a structural job rather than a hat, holds its shape from two events to ten, and lands a call to action at the base. What every direction holds to

  • Day colour leads. Not a header band — a flood, a misprint, a full-height spine, a stub pair, or a repeating time chip.
  • Radius 0, 2px ink, flat down-shadow. No rounding, no glow.
  • Montserrat names, Space Grotesk states facts. Times, titles and rooms are all Grotesk; only the day name and labels take Montserrat caps.
  • Mandatory furniture. Wordmark in a box, 86 Mai Thúc Lân, the real QR to app.realitydn.com.

How the CTA gives way

The brief says the call to action stands down on crowded days. That's a ladder, not a switch, and it is the same in all five:

2–7
Full. Sentence, wordmark, address and a 128px code.
8–9
Compact. One line, 96px code, address folded in.
10
Minimal. Wordmark, bare host, 72px code. The sentence goes.

What isn't decided here

Banner emphasis, closed-day states and the 4:5 feed crop are drawn from the chosen direction, not chosen alongside it — each of the five has an obvious path for all three, and pinning them now would only be guessing at a layout that hasn't been picked.
