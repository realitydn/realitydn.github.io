The audit is closed — 19.08.26

F1 · Day surface ladder → two steps off-paper. The earlier revert pick is withdrawn; --surface-2 #f4ecd7 and --paper-shade #ece2c9 stand as shipped. Day headers, poster placeholders and skeletons keep their surface. No code change.

F2 · ACTION button → cream on red, knowingly. It measures 4.19:1 and fails AA, and it is now the only coloured fill in the system not carrying ink — taken deliberately, with the measurement in hand, because red reads as a filled ink button at distance. Recorded as an exception, not an oversight: it is written into reality-tokens.json under openCalls so nobody “fixes” it silently, and it does not generalise — info, notice and every day-code fill still take ink.

F3 · Partner map canon, never-pair advisory. The seven pairs are the enforced derivation; the four discouraged combinations warn rather than block, so a deliberate near-tonal overprint stays available at poster scale. day-colours.json now carries neverPairEnforcement: "warn".

F4 · Tracking ladder baked per role. The six-number optical ladder is ratified; no size-derived formula. The print and signage offsets ride on top.
