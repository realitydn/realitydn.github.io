B — Contradictions · the system currently says two things

Is pink the default accent, or a minor? The palette card says “on surfaces, lead with the three majors” and, in the same table, names pink — a minor — as the default accent. --accent is the most-repeated colour signal in the product: every focus ring, every input focus, the bottom-nav marker, and the deeper of the two misregistration prints behind every hero. → Decide the rule, then make the palette card and this one agree.

App and website ship the accent pair inverted. The website sets --accent: blue with pink as the echo. The app sets --accent: pink with blue as the echo. Neither overrides it per-context anywhere, despite both describing it as “swappable”. One of them is wrong, or the token is theme-aware and neither has implemented that. → See Colour Hierarchy for the three options, rendered live.

The day-code carries no rank, and it outweighs everything else. Seven days, seven hues, equal weight; four are minors, including the busiest night. Measured across both home pages, minors take 63% of the on-palette colour in the app and 54% on the site. The hierarchy rule and the day-code are both canon and they pull against each other. → Rank by hue, rank by treatment, or accept the day-code as a separate axis and say so.

Theme-flip duration. The Surfaces card says the flip settles in ~350ms. The app ships 700ms (--dur-enter) via an html.theme-settling class that transitions every surface, border, shadow and fill together, then releases back to interaction timing. → 700ms is shipped and feels right; the card needs correcting, unless 350ms was deliberate.

Surfaces card asserts a rule the app has retired. Its subtitle still reads “in Day, bg = surface = surface-2”. That was true when --surface-2 was collapsed onto paper; it is not true now. → Rewrite the subtitle and the Day column once section A is accepted.

ACTION buttons are ink-on-red, not cream-on-red. Decided 17.08.26 on the one button that converts: cream-on-red measured 4.19:1 at button size, under AA. Ink lands 4.6:1 and matches how info and notice already carry ink. The Button component here has not been checked against this. → Verify components/actions/Button.jsx and its prompt.

C — Omissions · shipped in the app, absent from canon

Priority is my read, not a decision
High
Misregister as a live layer

DNA rule 4 is stated as a principle but has no spec. The app implements it as two drifting pseudo-layers (.hero-echo, .cal-hero-wrap) on desynced 9s / 13s loops, with larger offsets above 640px. Requires isolation:isolate on a background-less ancestor or the negative-z layer vanishes — a gotcha that has bitten twice.

High
Riso paper grain

A fixed fractal-noise wash over the whole page — multiply at 4% in Day, screen at 5.5% in Night. Signature texture, zero markup, and the cheapest “printed stock” win in the system. Not documented anywhere here.

High
Calendar feed family

.cal-card, .cal-panes, .cal-slice, .cal-daymat, .cal-datetab, .cal-ov, .cal-bx-*, .cal-go, .cal-hero-wrap. The most-seen component set in the product has no card and no primitive here.

Med
Button role variants

.btn-action (red / imperative), .btn-info (blue), .btn-notice (yellow), .btn-alt (paper). Where the majors are supposed to earn their volume — currently 53 / 4 / 2 uses against 249 structural buttons.

Med
Type role classes

.type-display (weight 100, clamp 40–82), .type-h1, .type-h2, .type-h3, .type-label, .type-page. The typography card documents the roles; the app has them as real classes with locked clamps.

Med
12px informational floor

Decided 17.08.26: date chips and time·room lines are deciding facts read in a dark bar, so 12px is the floor for informational text. Decorative labels may stay at 11px.

Low
The motion library

The motion card documents curves and durations. The app ships ~90 named keyframes built on them — stamp-in, chip-pop, plate wipes, reprint pass, quad seal, slur, ken push, promo reel. Worth an index card at minimum, so they get reused instead of reinvented.

Low
Smaller primitives

.live-dot, .tag, .chip-dot, .skeleton (with its ink sheen), .lift, .marquee, .field-label / .field-hint, and the view-transition hooks .vt-hero / .vt-title / .vt-date.

D — Studio-era conflicts · found folding in the website generators

Poster · Schedule · Print Studio vs this system · 17.08.26

The website is a third answer on the corrected tokens. Section A above is shipped in the app and now written here. The website still carries the pre-fix values: --fg-faint at .45 / .32, --surface-2 collapsed onto paper, Day shadows at .08 / .12 / .18. Same three tokens, three different values across app, site and system. → Not a decision — the site needs the section A patch applied.

--accent-2 “defaults to blue” versus a partner map. All three studios derive the second ink from a fixed warm/cool pairing — pink→blue, red→blue, blue→pink, yellow→pink, amber→purple, green→purple, purple→amber — implemented identically in the riso engine and the print exporter. The token sheet says the second layer defaults to blue, which is only true for two of the seven. → Promote the partner map to canon and reword the token, or state that screen UI and print artwork use different rules.

Print shadows are still the pre-correction values. Print Studio’s lift steps are .08 / .12 / .18 with heavy at 12px, citing the style guide. The app corrected Day to .10 / .16 / .23 at 13px because cream washed the lighter values out — a screen-specific reason that may not apply to a K tint on white stock. → Either print is explicitly exempt (say so in the token comment) or the values are stale. Right now it reads as drift.

Six treatments documented, thirteen shipped. Spot, dither, hatch, photocopy, contour, edges and mosaic exist in the engine with full dial sets and no card here — and the treatment list in Photo Treatments no longer matches what the picker offers. → Evolution, not a contradiction — but the Photo Treatments card is now wrong rather than merely incomplete.

Two site strings. Posters and the schedule footer print realitydn.com. Print Studio calls www.realitydn.com a mandatory brand string. Both are described as canon in their own file. → Pick one for artwork. (The QR encodes the bare host either way.)

Print ink and paper are off-palette by design, undocumented as such. Ink is #111111 not #0d0905; the sheet is white, and cream is never laid down as a fill. Both are correct press decisions and neither is written anywhere in the system, so they look like mistakes to anyone diffing hexes. → Add a substrate rule to the palette card: these values are the print equivalents, not a second opinion.

“Never hardcode a weekday hue” cannot hold for canvas renderers. The Schedule Studio ships the seven weekday colours as hex literals, because a canvas/PDF renderer cannot read the app’s CSS custom properties. The rule as written has no exemption, so the tool that leans on day-coding hardest is formally in violation. → Reword the rule for renderers, or ship the mapping as JSON both sides import.

Type: three scales, no stated relationship. The system documents type roles (Display at weight 100, H1…H2, label, body). The studios snap to modular scales instead — px 18→248 on canvas, pt 7→128 in print — and nothing maps a role onto a step. → Decide whether roles govern artwork at all, or are a UI-only contract.

5:7 is gone. The June revision lists 5:7 as a poster reflow target; the shipped output set is 4:5, 1:1, 9:16 and A4, with 5:7 and the FB cover removed because 4:5 serves FB events. → Fixed 18.08.26 — REVISION.md corrected; the Studios card reflects the shipped set.
