What is already in

Two things landed before this card, because they needed no decision. The canon mark is on the schedule: the full 9×2 strip on the masthead beside ĐÀ NẴNG, and the square-anchored square riding the footer QR flush, module pinned to whichever of the QR's pattern or tile actually reads as its edge, so the two match on every palette. And the QR now targets app.realitydn.com, not the apex — a listing's natural partner is the live version of itself, where every event has a detail page and a sheet printed on Monday still resolves to Thursday's changes. It carries a caption sized to the block: Full schedule + event details where there is room, Full schedule where there is less, nothing under ~52px. The printed site string stays realitydn.com.

What is not settled is everything below: which look actually survives 40 events, where the mark belongs, what a day plate should be, and whether a schedule is read near or far.

Three things the table says out loud

1 · Ledger is the only look that survives the real week. At 38 it is tight everywhere but nothing overflows; at 40 the same. Stack breaks on the feed at 40, Grid breaks on more.

2 · Grid is already broken on WhatsApp — at 30 events, today. That is not a 40-event problem, it is a live bug: the 1:1 card cannot hold seven modular day cells at any volume we actually programme. It has presumably been shipping clipped.

3 · "Tight" is the normal state, not the exception. From 35 events up, every look on every channel is running at the bottom of its type ladder. There is no headroom left to spend on ink chrome — a plate, a rule or a mark has to be paid for out of something else. That is what S3 and S5 are about.

Masthead

The full 9×2 strip sits beside ĐÀ NẴNG, bare — no ground, because the sheet is already stock. The 7×2 short form is the fallback for a masthead too narrow to hold nine cells. Module pinned to the ĐÀ NẴNG cap height so the two hold the same weight at every channel scale.

Footer

Square-anchored, flush against the QR. On a light ground (Day · Press · Paper) the code's quiet zone vanishes into the sheet, so the square matches its pattern; on Night and Carbon the cream tile reads as a rectangle a third larger, so it matches the tile. Its ink cell takes the outer corner, so it needs no ground on any substrate.

It mirrors the poster exactly — strip at the top of the composition, square on the brand-carrying footer — so a poster and a schedule pinned side by side read as one system. And it is the arrangement already running, so choosing A is choosing to keep it.

CostTwo marks on one sheet, which needs the canon footnote every time someone counts them.

BFooter only
Rule

No masthead strip. The square riding the QR is the schedule's only mark — literally one per surface, no footnote needed.

Effect

The masthead is wordmark · ĐÀ NẴNG · date stamp, exactly as it is today.

The strictest reading of the canon, and it gives back masthead width — which at 38 events is width the date stamp could use.

CostThe mark only appears at the very bottom of a sheet people read from the top. On the 9:16 story, the footer is the part that gets cropped in a repost.

Rule

The channel registry gains a register field. print · wa → near (sentence case, Grotesk names). stories · feed · daily · cover → far (Montserrat caps names). Times stay Grotesk with tabular figures in both — facts never change family (M1).

It is the merged system applied literally, and the channel registry is exactly the "closed list of surfaces" M2 asked for — no metres, no size threshold, one field per channel. It also matches how the two are actually consumed: the A4 is held, the story is thumbed past.

S5 · What gives at 40

S5From 35 events up, every look on every channel is at the bottom of its ladder. What is the release valve?Not a styling question — this is what happens on the week we programme 40 and something has to go.Needs a call
ASplit sooner, automaticallySuggested
Rule

The two-column A4 and the carousel already split. Lower the trigger: above 36 events the sheet splits by default rather than compressing, and the split point is chosen by bestSplit as it is today.

Effect

A 40-event week prints as two A4s or a 2-part carousel at comfortable type, instead of one sheet at the ladder floor.

Trades a constraint we do not control (how many events we run) for one we do (how many sheets we print). Nothing is dropped, nothing shrinks, and it uses machinery that already exists and is already tested.

CostTwo sheets by the door instead of one, and two slides where people expect one image. On WhatsApp a second card is a real cost — people forward the first.

BShort titles kick in
WED10.6 16:30PULSE 2: Ambition2L 18:30Short Films Talk2E
The titleShort field, used automatically instead of only at ladder step ≤ 1.
Rule

Above 36 events the renderer prefers titleShort wherever one exists, at any ladder step — rather than waiting until the type has already collapsed.

The short titles are already written and already in the data; today they only appear once things are desperate. Using them earlier keeps type big AND keeps one sheet.

CostOnly helps where a short title exists — currently about a third of events — so a heavy week of long unshortened names gains nothing. And it hides detail on exactly the weeks with the most on.

CExtend the ladder down
Rule

Add one or two smaller steps below the current floor, so "tight" has somewhere left to go before "over".

Against canon

C6 type floor: anything you would act on — times, rooms, prices, dates — sits at 12px or above. The print ladder already bottoms at 12.5px.

Simplest possible change, no layout work, no extra sheets.

CostIt breaks a ratified floor, on the surface where the floor matters most — a wall schedule read standing up. This option is here to be rejected on the record, so nobody proposes it again in six months.

Shipped without a vote

The mark is on the schedule (masthead short strip · square riding the footer QR, both bare, module pinned to the QR pattern). The QR targets app.realitydn.com with a length-aware caption, on every channel — Feed, Stories and WhatsApp had none at all, so the app link reached only the A4 sheet. On the carousels it rides the footer margin beside the centred stack, which costs no height: the capacity table above is unchanged by it. The ink-mark spec was ported into schedule-data.jsx in the canonical shape and added to tools/verify-day-colours.mjs, so the schedule's mark cannot drift from the poster's or the print one. Say the word if any of those should be opened up instead.

Not shipped, and not a decision: Grid's WhatsApp overflow is a bug at every volume we programme. It gets fixed regardless of which option S1 takes.
