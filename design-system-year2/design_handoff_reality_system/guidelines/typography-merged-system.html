Why they compose

5 answers which family: Montserrat names things, Space Grotesk states facts. 6 answers which case: caps for what's read across a room, sentence case for what's read at arm's length. Neither touches the other's variable — so the merged rule is two independent axes, and every role in the system is one cell of that grid. Poster titles stay all-caps (far + name), schedule names lose caps (near + name), schedule times move to Grotesk (near + fact). That is exactly what you asked for.

Confirmed on the way in: Space Grotesk carries the Vietnamese subset — I measured ĐÊM NHẠC MỞ / ộ / ữ against fallback in both families and both render natively. Moving facts to Grotesk does not cost VI coverage.

What's missing — eight gaps

Call 1 · "Near" and "far" aren't metres

A website has no viewing distance. The rule has to name surface classes, or it will be argued about forever — and the same weekly schedule exists as an A4 print, an app list, and a TV behind the bar, which is one content type in three distance classes.

Call 2 · Grotesk's own tracking

The optical ladder was derived from Montserrat's wide geometric caps. Grotesk is narrower, has real lowercase, and wants roughly 0 to −.01em — applying Montserrat's numbers to it would look sprayed.

Call 3 · Artist and proper names

Sentence-case names raise a question caps hid: bảo anh, MIDNIGHT WOLVES, d.j. Hương — do we normalise performers' own capitalisation or preserve it? Caps used to erase the problem.

Ratify 4 · Figures

Tabular figures for anything in a column — times, prices, capacities, scores — proportional everywhere else. Plus the money format: 45.000₫, dot thousands, symbol trailing, no space.

Call 5 · Schedule overflow

Sentence case buys ~12% width, but "Language Exchange · Beginner Table" still overruns a phone. Truncate with an ellipsis, wrap to two lines, or cap titles at a character budget at entry time?

Ratify 6 · Vietnamese

line-height ≥ 1.28 on any uppercase VI, and caps VI never at weight 100 — stacked marks above cap-height collide or vanish. Sentence-case VI is safe at 1.15.

Call 7 · Print tracking

Ink spread closes counters, so print wants roughly +.01em over screen and signage +.02em. Either the ladder gets per-medium sets, or print stays on screen values and we accept slightly tighter caps.

Call 8 · The two-brands risk

Under 5+6 the app title reads Open Mic Night and the poster reads OPEN MIC. Deliberate — but it needs a stated reason on the card, or it will be "fixed" by someone in six months.
