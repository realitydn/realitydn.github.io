Keyboard focus — four candidates

Focus was never designed for the Year 2 system, so every prototype has been inheriting the browser default: a rounded blue ring. Wrong on all three counts — it has a radius, blue is a major carrying wayfinding, and it is nobody's decision.

Each candidate is :focus-visible only (a mouse press never draws it), zero radius, and not reused for selected or active anywhere else. Every one is shown on the six controls that stress it: an ink button, the red ACTION, a chip, the tab bar, a bare text link, a coloured quiz plate, and an input. Day above, Night below.

C is chosen (22.08.26) and is now in the tokens as canon — see focus in reality-tokens.json. The other three stay rendered as the record of what was weighed.
