FocusKeyboard focus was never designed for Year 2, so every surface inherited the browser default — a rounded blue ring, wrong on all three counts: it has a radius, blue is a major carrying wayfinding, and it was nobody's decision.CANON · 22.08.26

Definition

A misregister at control scale. Two impressions of the same shape, one slipped — the same move as the hero plates, applied to a button. Focus reads as a printing accident, not as a UI ring.

Chosen over three alternatives (offset ink outline, inset ink ring, accent slab) because it is the only candidate already in the system's DNA rather than a new idea, and because it is theme-aware for free.

  transform: translate(-4px,-4px);

Two properties. No pseudo-element, no wrapper, no z-index.

The face separates from the colour under it

The control is two parts: a solid accent plate holding the button's real position, and the face — border, fill, label — sliding off it. If the whole control translated, nothing would have misregistered; it would just have moved. That distinction is the entire idea.

02 · Why box-shadow and not a plate element

Never — a ::after plate at z-index:-1

It needs isolation: isolate on a background-less ancestor. Put isolate on the element carrying the fill and the negative-z child paints after that fill — the plate covers the button and only a 4px sliver of face shows. Drop isolate instead and the plate falls behind the band background and vanishes silently. Both halves of this trap are already flagged in CLAUDE.md for .misreg; both were hit here.

Always — a spread-less box-shadow

A box-shadow paints behind the element's own background by definition. The face fill can never be covered, the plate always lands on the original box, and there is no stacking context to get wrong. 4px 4px 0 0 — offset, no blur, no spread. It replaces the control's rest shadow while focused, which is correct: the plate is the shadow slot.

03 · The plate never shares a family with the surface

On blueField blueplate takes pink
On yellowField yellowplate takes red
On redField redplate takes yellow
On paperField paperplate takes the accent — blue in Day, pink in Night

This is the accent rule, re-declared per band

Day leads blue and the wayfinding band is blue, so on that band the plate prints blue-on-blue and disappears. The palette already forbids an accent sharing a family with its surface; the only new work is re-declaring --accent on each coloured band. Day-code fields take ink.

05 · Rules

:focus-visible only

A mouse press never draws it. Bare :focus gets outline: none.

Never reused

Not for selected, active, hover or current. Focus is the only thing this pattern means.

The face must be opaque

A transparent control lets the plate show through and it reads as a fill. Faces that already carry a fill keep it.

List rows need 4px trailing

The plate extends 4px down-right; without room it laps the next row.

Zero radius

Both the face and the plate. As everywhere.

Reduced motion

Drop the transition, keep the offset. The slipped state is the information.
