Running
the shift

You're the person who confirms the place is genuinely in good shape.

Two jobs come with that, and both of them pay.

You earn points the same way everyone does — read the staff guide first. This is the extra bit.

01

Claim the shift

At the top of your screen there's a bar that says Shift lead.

Shift lead Claim it · +14 on the board

One person holds it per shift. If someone's beaten you to it, you'll see their name.

Claiming puts two jobs on your list that belong to you alone. That's the +14.

Handing over early? Tap hand over on the same bar and the next lead can claim it.

02

Your two walkthroughs

Opening walkthrough (shift lead)
Walk the whole building. Look at what the last shift marked done and confirm it. Note anything broken, missing or dirty, so you start the shift knowing the state of everything.

03

Give people their jobs

Dashboard → Assign.

Pick a person at the bottom, tap jobs to give them to that person. Tap a section heading to give them the whole section.

Each job shows its points while you're assigning, so you can see how the load sits across the team.

Sweep and mop — 2E 12
Minh · Linh · ÷2

Give a job to two people and it shows ÷2 — they'll split the points.

Unassigned jobs stay open for anyone to pick up, which suits the fast-moving work.

Someone asks for a job with another person's name on it? Move it to them on this screen, or add them so they share it.

04

Checking people's work

This is the important part.

Every finished job shows who did it and how long ago. Underneath you get three buttons.

✓

⏱ 2h 14m · Minh
8
OK

You looked, it's good. Use this one a lot — good work deserves a mark on it.

Forgive

You're looking at it hours after they did it, and the place has moved on since. Somebody mopped at 18:00, it's 22:00, and a busy Friday happened in between.

Forgiving keeps their points. Reach for it whenever the hours are what made the mess.

Void

It got ticked while the job was still waiting.

  1. Tap Void — your camera opens
  2. Take a photo of the job as it stands
  3. The points come off and the photo goes to their phone

The job returns to the list, and doing it properly pays the full amount. A void is a re-do.

The rule of thumb

Void — the job is still waiting.

Forgive — the job got done, and time moved on.

Someone else always checks your work, the same way you check theirs.

05

Close the shift

Dashboard → Review → Close shift.

The review screen shows everything from the shift: who did what, when, and what it paid. Fix anything that looks wrong before you close.

Closing locks the shift. Everything after that belongs to the next one.

If a closed shift needs opening again, a manager can do it.
