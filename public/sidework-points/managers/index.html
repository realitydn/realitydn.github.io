Running
the game

Managers run the game. The crew competes in it.

You sit outside the points entirely — the app leaves you out of splits automatically — and that's exactly what makes every call you make a clean one.

The whole system exists to reward the people who keep REALITY looking and feeling great. Everything here keeps that true.

01

Checking work —
the timer is the point

Every finished job, anywhere in the app, shows who did it and how long ago.

✓
1F Women's made shiny
⏱ 2h 14m · Minh
8

Someone says "I just cleaned that."

The row already says 2h 14m. The number settles it, on their screen as well as yours.

OK

Looked at it, it's good. Worth using often — good work deserves a mark on it.

Forgive

Looking at it hours later, the place has moved on since. They keep their points.

Void

Ticked while the job was still waiting. Camera opens, photograph it, points come off, photo goes to their phone, job returns to the list.

Reach for Forgive often. It keeps the system fair when hours pass, and it's what gives a Void its weight on the rare occasion you use one.

A bathroom that's still dirty? Void it right from your own screen, standing there.

02

Giving a bonus

Dashboard → Award a bonus.

Award a bonus
+5 +10 +20 +50
Reason (required — it's on the record)

Pick a person, pick an amount, write a reason. The reason stays on the record with your name.

Good uses: a great set of event photos, someone clearing a mess that came from elsewhere, a shift where somebody obviously carried it.

03

One-off jobs

Dashboard → Extra items. Add a job for a specific date and shift, with points on it.

Mold patrol
Walk the walls, behind the furniture, the window frames, the bathroom ceilings and the door edges. Photograph anything you find and report it.
6
Mold treatment EXTRA
Treat what patrol found, properly, and check back that it's gone.
22

Patrol pays 6 because it's a look. Treating it is real work, so it goes on as its own job for whoever takes it.

Same for anything that comes up: a delivery to break down, a room to strip.

04

Changing what
things are worth

app.realitydn.com/admin/sidework/tasks

Two numbers matter per job:

PointsWhat it's worth normally
Cadence hoursHow long until it's back to full price. Past that it keeps climbing, up to 2.5×

There's also What it means — the plain-words description under each job on everyone's phone. That's the same text printed in the staff guide, so this is the one place to edit it.

Leave the rest of that screen as it is.

05

Reading the month

app.realitydn.com/control/sidework — the Points panel at the top.

ColumnWhat it tells you
PointsMonth total. Top two are your payout.
TapsHow many jobs. Big points on few taps means they take the heavy work.
HalfHow often they hit the slow-down rule
VoidsHow many of their jobs got voided

Underneath, voids issued per judge — who's doing the stamping. A lead who voids a lot surfaces here on their own.

Month one: watch half-rate

Before points, nearly every shift got logged in one sitting at the end of the night. If half-rate comes back high across everybody, the rule is set too tight — loosen the rule.

07

Adding someone new

app.realitydn.com/admin/sidework — add them by their Google email, set a name, colour and role.

RoleCan
StaffDo jobs, see standings
Team leadPlus claim the shift, assign, check work, void, close
ManagerPlus bonuses, extras, reopening — and steps outside the points

Sit with them while they sign in the first time. That first sign-in on their own phone is what turns a roster row into a player — it's what lets them tick jobs off, appear in standings, and get notified.
