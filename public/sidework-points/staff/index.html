How the

REALITY works because of how it feels to walk into. Clean glass. A bathroom that smells fresh. Tables ready before anyone sits down.

That feeling is the product, and the people on shift build it every single day.

Sidework is the list of those jobs, on your phone. Every job carries points. Do the work, take the points.

At the end of every month, the two people with the most points get a bonus.

1st place
1,000,000đ
2nd place
250,000đ

Then everyone goes back to zero, and the next month belongs to whoever wants it.

The idea is simple. The people who do the most to keep this place great are the people who get rewarded for it.

05

Jobs get worth more
the longer they wait

This is the good part. When a job sits untouched, it starts paying more — up to two and a half times more.

The couches are normally 22. They've been waiting a month, so today they're 55.

Couch wipedown
Wipe all the fabric with the right cleaner, and get the crumbs and rubbish out of the cushions.
55
bounty ×2.5

When a job climbs above its normal price the number turns pink. First person to it takes the lot.

So the ugly jobs are often the richest ones on the list. Worth a scan before you pick.

It runs the other way too — a job finished ten minutes ago resets to near zero, then climbs back over the next few hours.

07

Weeklies

Under Weeklies are the bigger rotation jobs — the storage room, the mirrors, the grinder. They run on an eight-day clock, so they're usually carrying the fattest numbers on your screen.

They're pure upside. Take one when you have a minute.

08

Working with someone

Bar station full clean
You + one other · 40 ÷ 2
20

If a job is assigned to both of you, tapping it splits the points evenly. The app handles that part.

Unassigned jobs are open to whoever gets there first.

If a job carries someone else's name, ask your shift lead — they'll move it across in a second.

09

Take your time

Tap six jobs inside five minutes, and the next five minutes pay half.

Easy tiger · 4 of 5

The points are for the work, and the work takes time. An orange bar fills up on your screen as you go, so you can watch it coming.

Closing jobs stay at full price all night. Everyone finishes together at the end, and that's exactly how it should be.
